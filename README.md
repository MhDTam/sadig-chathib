# Truth or Bluff · صدگ چذب

**Speak with confidence. Don’t get caught.**

[**▶ Play now**](https://sadig-chathib.onrender.com) · [العربية](README.ar.md)

An Iraqi card game for 2–4 players, built around bluffing, challenges, and ceramic-cup penalties. Baghdad, Basra, and Diyala cards bring a local identity to a palace-style table. Play with friends or face three bots in your browser.

[![Truth or Bluff — gameplay in English](media/gameplay-en.png)](https://sadig-chathib.onrender.com)

## The game

- **Friends or bots:** create a table, invite friends, join with a code, or start a practice match.
- **A different kind of bluff:** province cards, Iraq wild cards, and a changing round card.
- **Arabic & English:** choose your language without leaving the game.
- **Desktop & mobile:** animated cards, sound effects, and adjustable timers and cups.

## How to play

1. Open [the game](https://sadig-chathib.onrender.com), enter a nickname, and choose how to play.
2. Each player starts with five cards. Play 1–3 cards face down and claim they match the round card; Iraq cards act as wild cards.
3. On your turn, play cards or call the previous claim a bluff. A wrong claim or challenge costs a cup. After a challenge, active players receive five fresh cards and a new round card is chosen. Be the last player with cups remaining.
